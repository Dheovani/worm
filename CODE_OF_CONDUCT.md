# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Worm to be a respectful, constructive, and low-ego place to build useful
software. Contributors and maintainers are expected to participate with empathy,
patience, and a willingness to explain technical decisions clearly.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and welcoming.
- Assume good intent, but accept responsibility for impact.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Prefer clear explanations over sarcasm or gatekeeping.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Help newcomers understand the codebase without making them feel small.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make the project unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or limit
participation when behavior violates this code of conduct. Enforcement should be
proportional, documented when appropriate, and focused on protecting the health
of the community.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project
maintainers privately when possible. If private contact is not available yet,
open a GitHub issue with only the minimum public information needed and ask for
a maintainer contact path.

Reports will be handled with care and confidentiality whenever possible.
